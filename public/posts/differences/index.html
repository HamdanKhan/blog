Differences

Saturday, February 8, 2025

These days I find myself choosing to pray Friday prayers behind someone who, due to their school of thought, would likely not pray behind me or the Imam of my school, if roles were reversed. But that is ok.

Growing up, my approach to religious practice was firmly rooted in what I had been taught, not by my family but by neighbors, friends, and the local masjid. I restricted myself to praying only in mosques affiliated with my specific school of thought, creating invisible boundaries that limited my spiritual experience. Looking back, I can see how my perspective has evolved. 

The turning point in my journey began with the masjid near my home, a different neighborhood, a different masjid with slight variations from my traditional school of thought, circumstances led me to join their Quranic classes. What started as a hesitant step evolved into a transformative experience as I completed a year-long course. This decision, however, meant spending less time with my original community’s activities, creating an internal conflict I needed to navigate. 

The course I mentioned last, delved into these fundamental texts and differences of opinion, these days people encourage studying them directly, rather than relying solely on interpretations, my perspective is still that you can not let go of the interpretation of scholars who were close to the source. Also, I have not spent much time exploring them myself, something to be altered.

Today, I find myself praying at a masjid near my office – one that I would have previously avoided due to its different school of thought. This evolution in my practice wasn’t sudden.

That said, I cannot accept any abuse toward the elders that came before. I realize that there were differences after the death of Prophet Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) and a few generations behind them, but any step in unifying the ummah and being considerate to people of all faiths, starting with those closest to you first, and the community and the masjids have a vital role to play in this.
